### [1] [Descent, Seminormalization, and Conductors of Sparse Profile Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12854)
*Yangcheng Li*

Main category: math.AG

TL;DR: 本文为稀疏因子分解轮廓像建立下降理论，研究哪些正规化上的函数能下降到实际像，并对齐次掩码、混合非周期轮廓及单点-补轮廓给出分类与显式计算。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者希望理解稀疏因子分解轮廓像的下降问题：判断正规化（normalization）上的哪些函数能够下降到实际像，并刻画实际像代数及其相关不变量（如导子、深度、几何纤维等）。

Method: 建立稀疏因子分解轮廓像的下降理论；利用整纤维完备化得到齐次掩码的 Fourier-zero 分类及尖锐稳定逆定理；对混合非周期轮廓给出 Fourier-line 分类；对有界整数关系刻画几何纤维与半正规化；用显式半群模型计算实际像代数、导子与深度；对单点-补轮廓用精确一阶 jet 条件确定有限输出代数、导子及射影下降；并证明饱和单位根特化定理。

Result: 获得齐次掩码的 Fourier-zero 分类与尖锐稳定逆定理；在指定标记稳定范围内得到混合非周期轮廓的 Fourier-line 分类；对若干大类计算出实际像代数、导子与深度；确定单点-补轮廓的有限输出代数、导子及第一非正规边界上的射影下降，并在光滑边界范围与第一非正规层全零点计算局部类型；同时构造分离性例子。

Conclusion: 正规化、自然极化、点纤维、半正规化乃至源导子都不一定能决定实际像；本文的下降理论与分类结果刻画了实际像的确定条件，并给出相应显式计算与分离例子。

Abstract: We develop a descent theory for sparse factorization-profile images, asking which functions on a normalization descend to the actual image. Whole-fiber completions yield a Fourier-zero classification for homogeneous masks, with a sharp stable converse, and a Fourier-line classification for mixed aperiodic profiles in the stated marked stable ranges. For homogeneous profiles, bounded integer relations determine geometric fibers and seminormalization, while explicit semigroup models compute actual image algebras, conductors, and depth in substantial families. For singleton--complement profiles, exact one-jet conditions determine the finite-output algebra, conductor, and projective descent through the first nonnormal boundary; the local type is computed in the smooth-boundary range and at the total-zero point of the first nonnormal layer. We also prove a saturated root-of-unity specialization theorem and give separating examples showing that normalization, natural polarizations, point fibers, seminormalization, and even the source conductor need not determine the actual image.
