Main category: math.AG

TL;DR: 本文证明了从固定亏格曲线到射影簇的态射空间的紧支撑Betti数具有一致指数界，并将其应用于高亏格函数域上的Manin猜想（分裂四次del Pezzo曲面情形）。

Motivation: 作者旨在将Das-Lehmann-Tanimoto-Tosteson在亏格0情形下的结果推广到高亏格，建立高亏格函数域版本的四次del Pezzo曲面Manin猜想，并给出具有预期前导常数的渐近式。

Result: 建立了态射空间紧支撑Betti数的一致指数上界，该界关于态射次数呈指数增长，且不依赖于基域、源曲线和目标；并据此在高亏格函数域上证明了分裂四次del Pezzo曲面的Manin猜想，在足够大的有限域上且将曲线类限制在略收缩的nef锥内时，获得预期渐近式和正确的领头常数。

Conclusion: 该工作将Manin猜想的函数域证明从亏格0推广到任意固定亏格，为更一般的射影簇上的计数问题提供了统一方法。

Abstract: We prove uniform exponential bounds for the compactly supported Betti numbers of spaces of morphisms from curves of fixed genus to projective varieties. For targets in a fixed projective space cut out by a prescribed number of equations of fixed degrees, the bound is exponential in the degree of the morphism and is independent of the ground field, the source curve, and the target. The proof constructs bounded-degree affine presentations involving only linearly many variables and equations, and then applies Katz's estimate.
  As an application, we establish a higher genus function field version of Manin's conjecture for split quartic del Pezzo surfaces, generalizing a recent theorem of Das--Lehmann--Tanimoto--Tosteson. Over sufficiently large finite fields, and after restricting curve classes to a slightly shrunken nef cone, we obtain the predicted asymptotic with the expected leading constant. As in Das--Lehmann--Tanimoto--Tosteson's argument, we combine the uniform Betti bound with a higher genus homological sieve, a bar complex calculation, and a virtual height zeta function.

### [31] [On Manin's conjecture for quartic del Pezzo fibrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28471)
*Sho Tanimoto*

Main category: math.AG

TL;DR: 本文推广了同调筛法，用于研究四次del Pezzo纤维化，并在全局函数域上证明了Manin猜想的一个版本。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将Das、Lehmann、Tosteson和作者先前发展的同调筛法推广到更一般的四次del Pezzo纤维化情形，特别是Picard秩为2、没有锥纤维化的情形。

Method: 结合三维正特征极小模型程序（MMP）、截面空间与Abel-Jacobi映射的几何，以及同调筛法三种工具。

Result: 在全局函数域上证明了这些四次日del Pezzo纤维化的Manin猜想版本。

Conclusion: 同调筛法可以被推广到不具有锥纤维化结构的四次日del Pezzo曲面情形，从而在更广泛的背景下验证Manin猜想。

Abstract: We generalize the homological sieve method, developed by Das, Lehmann, Tosteson, and the author, to study certain quartic del Pezzo fibrations, and we prove a version of Manin's conjecture over global function fields in these cases. Our proofs combine the $3$-dimensional positive-characteristic minimal model program, the geometry of the space of sections and the Abel--Jacobi mapping, and the homological sieve method. Our quartic del Pezzo surfaces have Picard rank $2$, and admit two birational morphisms to non-split quadric surfaces. In particular, they do not possess any conic fibrations.
